AI Processing Disclosure

ClaimDesk uses computer vision and natural language AI models to accelerate claim intake. All AI-generated assessments are preliminary and subject to human adjuster review. Photo metadata (GPS, device info, timestamps) is automatically stripped before processing to protect policyholder privacy. Confidence scores indicate the AI model's certainty in each field assessment — scores below 70% are flagged for manual verification. Cross-check analysis compares the policyholder's written description against what the AI observes in photos to surface contradictions for adjuster attention.
